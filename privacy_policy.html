Privacy Policy for TallyLive

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

Around AI ("we", "our", or "us") operates the TallyLive mobile and web application. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and business information when using our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide and improve the TallyLive business analytics service:

  • Account Information: Full Name and 10-digit Mobile Number provided during user registration.
  • Business Analytics Data: Read-only business data synced from your authorized Tally ERP/Prime setup (e.g., stock valuations, sales summaries, daybook entries, receivables, and payables).
  • Device Information: Basic device metadata (OS version, app version) to ensure application compatibility and performance.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate business owners and authorize access to their specific business data.
  • To calculate and render real-time business reporting dashboards.
  • To prevent unauthorized access and ensure multi-tenant data privacy.

3. Data Protection & Security

Security of your data is paramount. TallyLive implements industry-standard Row-Level Security (RLS), HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, and secure authentication tokens. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third parties.

4. Account Deletion & Data Retention

Users may request deletion of their account profile and linked company records at any time by contacting support or requesting account deletion within the application settings.
